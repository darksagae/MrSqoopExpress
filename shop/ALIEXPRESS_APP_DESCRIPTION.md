## 📝 **BUSINESS NEEDS DESCRIPTION**

### Recommended for AliExpress Form:

```
MrSqoopExpress is a dropshipping e-commerce platform connecting African 
entrepreneurs to global suppliers through AliExpress. We enable resellers 
and small businesses to operate dropshipping stores without inventory 
investment or logistics complexity.

Our Platform:
- Aggregates quality products from AliExpress
- Provides affordable entry into e-commerce for African businesses
- Automates order processing and fulfillment
- Manages real-time inventory and pricing synchronization
- Serves customers across East Africa (currently Uganda)

Business Needs:
1. Access to AliExpress product database (search, browse, filtering)
2. Real-time product information updates (price, availability, images)
3. Automated order creation and processing capabilities
4. Inventory and stock level synchronization
5. Order status tracking and webhook notifications
6. Shipping information integration

We believe this partnership creates mutual benefit:
- For AliExpress: Increased sales volume from East African market
- For our users: Accessible e-commerce without capital barriers
- For customers: Affordable quality products with local payment options

Our first market is Uganda, with plans to expand to Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda.
```

## 💡 **ANSWERING SPECIFIC QUESTIONS**

### Q: "What is your main use case?"
```
Dropshipping marketplace that enables East African entrepreneurs to sell 
AliExpress products through custom storefronts with local payment options, 
without maintaining physical inventory.
```

### Q: "How will you use the API?"
```
1. Allow users to search and browse AliExpress products
2. Display products with custom pricing and local currency
3. Create orders when customers purchase
4. Forward orders to AliExpress for fulfillment
5. Track order status and notify customers
6. Monitor inventory in real-time
7. Receive webhook updates for order/inventory changes
```

### Q: "What is your business model?"
```
Reseller Commission Model:
- We add 25-40% markup to AliExpress prices
- Customers pay in local currency (UGX for Uganda)
- We profit from price difference minus payment fees and shipping
- No subscription fees - only profit per sale
```

### Q: "Who are your target customers?"
```
Primary: Small business owners and entrepreneurs (18-60 years old)
- Want to start online selling
- Limited capital for inventory
- Need simplified order fulfillment
- Based in East Africa

Secondary: Online stores looking to expand product catalog
```

### Q: "What volume do you expect?"
```
Phase 1 (Months 1-3): 100-500 orders/month
Phase 2 (Months 4-6): 500-2,000 orders/month
Phase 3 (Months 7-12): 2,000-5,000 orders/month
Phase 4 (Year 2+): 10,000+ orders/month

Estimated API calls:
Phase 1: 10,000-50,000 calls/month
Phase 4: 100,000+ calls/month
```

### Q: "How will you comply with AliExpress ToS?"
```
- We will only import legitimate product listings
- We will not engage in counterfeit or illegal products
- We will properly attribute products to AliExpress
- We will not scrape beyond API limits
- We will handle customer data securely
- We will follow all AliExpress policies
```

### Q: "What problem are you solving?"
```
Problem: Small African businesses want to sell online but lack:
- Capital to buy inventory
- Logistics expertise
- Access to reliable suppliers
- Local payment processing

Solution: MrSqoopExpress handles all of this through AliExpress integration
```
